About

Hi, I'm Ava. I'm currently a sophomore at Michigan State University pursuing dual degrees in Business Management and Informaiton Science.
I'm passionate about live music, connecting fan communities, and supporting artists through strong leadership.

Projects

Coffeeshop Concerts

In the Spring of 2023, I started a foundation called Coffeeshop Concerts: a concert series hosted in local cafes with goals of fundraising for youth music programs, providing performance experience for aspiring musicians, and bringing live music into the community.

Spartan Music Business Club

This fall, we have officially launched the Spartan Music Business Club.
We target professional development through a guest speaker series with industry professionals and hands-on experience through concert production and live music booking.
